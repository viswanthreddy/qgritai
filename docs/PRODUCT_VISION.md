# QgritAI Product Vision

## North star

QgritAI is the operating system for enterprise AI transformation: a premium, trustworthy platform that helps organizations discover, design, build, govern, and optimize AI initiatives.

## Product pillars

1. **Discover:** readiness, opportunity discovery, maturity scoring, ROI, and executive insight.
2. **Design:** roadmaps, solution blueprints, architecture, business cases, and proposals.
3. **Build:** AI agents, applications, workflow automation, integrations, and knowledge systems.
4. **Govern:** security, compliance, approvals, audit trails, and usage analytics.
5. **Optimize:** performance, cost, adoption, realized ROI, and continuous recommendations.

## Release sequence

1. Engineering, data, security, authentication, and design-system foundation.
2. Premium marketing platform and lead-generation tools.
3. Secure client collaboration workspace.
4. Internal CRM, proposals, projects, and reporting.
5. AI workflow, governance, marketplace, knowledge, and academy capabilities.

## Quality bar

Every released capability must be real, runnable, secure by default, accessible, responsive, testable, maintainable, and explicit about prototype boundaries. Unverified customer outcomes and placeholder functionality must never be presented as production capabilities.
