# QgritAI Service Website — Product Requirements

## Business goal

Present QgritAI as a founder-led, agent-powered AI transformation company that helps businesses understand, build, and operate practical AI capabilities. The website should create trust, explain the service model, demonstrate useful thinking, and convert qualified opportunities into direct conversations.

## Primary audiences

1. Business owners and executives who know AI matters but do not have a clear adoption path.
2. Functional leaders with expensive, repetitive, fragmented, or slow workflows.
3. Technology leaders evaluating agentic automation, AI applications, governance, or managed operations.
4. Growing companies seeking senior AI delivery without the overhead of a large consulting team.

## Public experience

- Founder-led, agent-powered positioning and operating model.
- Services covering strategy, workflow automation, agents, applications, governance, and managed AI operations.
- Industry workflow examples without unsupported expertise or outcome claims.
- Representative solution concepts clearly distinguished from verified client case studies.
- AI Readiness Assessment and Automation ROI Studio as directional opportunity tools.
- Direct contact journey focused on the business problem rather than a predetermined technical solution.
- Accessible, responsive, high-performance public pages with accurate metadata and indexing.

## Commercial journey

1. Visitor understands the problems QgritAI can solve.
2. Visitor explores a relevant service, industry pattern, concept, or planning tool.
3. Visitor shares a workflow, constraint, ambition, or strategic question.
4. Founder leads discovery and recommends an opportunity sprint, proof of value, implementation, managed service, or no-build alternative.

## Explicit non-goals for the public launch

- Selling a general-purpose SaaS platform.
- Requiring prospects or clients to use a project-management portal.
- Presenting an artificial team of human consultants.
- Claiming representative concepts are completed client engagements.
- Promising autonomous agents without human accountability, controls, and evaluation.
- Publishing unverified savings, performance, customer, or industry claims.

## Retained foundations

Authentication, tenant isolation, engagement workflows, documents, reporting, support, audit trails, and internal lead management remain available for future controlled use. They should become public product capabilities only when repeated client evidence demonstrates a clear need.
